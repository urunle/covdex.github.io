03 / Calculation method

From daily extremes
to one monthly mean.

For each date, the daily mean is estimated as the arithmetic midpoint between the observed maximum and minimum. The 31 daily estimates are then averaged to obtain one monthly value.

Formula used in this exercise
T̄month = (1 / 31) × Σ [(Tmax + Tmin) / 2]
The sum of the 31 daily midpoint temperatures is 984.2°C. Dividing by 31 gives 31.7484°C, rounded to 31.75°C.
STEP 01Collect the maximum and minimum temperatures for every day.
STEP 02Calculate each daily midpoint: (Tmax + Tmin) ÷ 2.
STEP 03Add all daily midpoints and divide by 31.
