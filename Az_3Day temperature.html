DATA QUALITY & INTERPRETATION

Temperatures, apparent temperatures, dew points and the October 14 peak gust are from the Iowa Environmental Mesonet daily archive. October 13 precipitation, maximum wind speed and visibility are from Weather Underground. The sources use different reporting methods and are not a single continuous sensor record. Missing values are labelled “Not reported”; they do not mean zero. Daily evaporation, humidity and atmospheric pressure could not be independently verified for all three dates, so they are not estimated. A station measurement is not necessarily identical to conditions at every location within Kerman.
