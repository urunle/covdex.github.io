02 / Analytical Method

Psychrometric equations for a ventilated wet-and-dry bulb mercury psychrometer.

Step 1 — Saturation vapor pressure
eₛ(T) = 0.61094 × exp(17.625T / (T + 243.04))

Step 2 — Actual vapor pressure
e = eₛ(Twb) − 0.00066(1 + 0.00115Twb)P(Tdb − Twb)

Step 3 — Relative humidity
RH = (e / eₛ(Tdb)) × 100

Step 4 — Dew point temperature
e = 0.61094 × exp(17.625Tdp / (Tdp + 243.04))

Step 5 — Humidity ratio
W = 0.62198 × e / (P − e)

03 / Graphical Method

Psychrometric chart: identify the state point using dry-bulb and wet-bulb temperatures. Compare the resulting humidity ratio and relative humidity with the analytical calculation.

Graphical interpretation
1. Locate the dry-bulb temperature on the horizontal axis.
2. Locate the wet-bulb temperature and follow its constant wet-bulb line.
3. The intersection gives the air state point.
4. Read the relative humidity curve and humidity ratio.
5. Follow the constant humidity-ratio line to the saturation curve to estimate dew point temperature.
